About me

I am Iana Vorushyna, a midwife (BSc). I support families through the first days and weeks with their baby – calmly, practically and without rigid rules.

My focus is postpartum care: I help you with breastfeeding, give you confidence in caring for your baby and look after your own recovery – including gentle postnatal exercises.

Since 2022 I have been working as a midwife at the Hirslanden Klinik im Park in Zurich, alongside caring for families in their homes as an independent midwife.

  • Midwife BSc, ZHAW Zurich University of Applied Sciences (2016)
  • Midwife at the Hirslanden Klinik im Park, Zurich (since 2022)
  • Around ten years of professional experience
  • Languages: German, English, Ukrainian, Russian
